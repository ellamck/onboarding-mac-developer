# Agile Ceremonies & Team Collaboration

## Research & Learn

**What are the main Agile ceremonies, and what is their purpose?**

All of these descriptions are taken from Radigan (2019):

Daily Stand-ups: These are short meetings that happen daily on work days where members in a team share progress, plans and any blockers they have.

Sprint Planning (Scrum) vs. Continuous Prioritization (Kanban)

- Sprint Planning is a meeting at the start of a sprint where the team plans what they will work on in the sprint and what they will achieve. They base this off of the product backlog (Radigan, 2019).
- In Kanban, the team always works on the most important tasks. I believe there is a weekly (or bi-weekly) meeting where the team goes through the backlog, adds any necessary items, and breaks down larger tasks. The team would also review the priorities of tasks.

Retrospectives: A meeting at the end of the sprint where the team discusses what was successful in the sprint and what they could work on in the next sprint.

Backlog Refinement: This ceremony is less documented, but this meeting is where the team meets to review and discuss items in the backlog. The priorities of tasks are reviewed, to ensure the Kanban board is reflective of the most important tasks.

**How do Agile teams collaborate asynchronously and across time zones?**

Agile teams can perform asynchronous ceremonies, for example, my student team at university performs asynchronous stand ups, writing them in Slack. Project management tools and documentation also help with collaboration, so each team member has a consistent point of reference. For development, Git and GitHub help with version control for asynchronous collaboration.

## Reflection

**How do Agile ceremonies help with communication and alignment?**

They create opportunities for the team to share progress and blockers, and allows the team to collaborate on prioritising tasks and gathering feedback. Agile ensures everyones working from the same backlog, with the same understanding of the product's vision.
