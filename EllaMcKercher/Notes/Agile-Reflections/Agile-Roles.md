# Roles in an Agile Team

## Research & Learn

**What are the key roles in an Agile team, and what does each role do?**

All of these role descriptions are taken from Vasiliauskas (2022).

Product Owner: This role defines the product's vision, liases and manages stakeholders, and based on the needs of the stakeholders and the product, prioritises the backlog.

Developers: This role builds the product, tests it and refines it.

QA (Quality Assurance): This role focuses on the testing of the product, and they work on the testing each lifecycle.

Product Manager (PM): Works with the product owner, this role also owns the roadmap and the strategy for the product (Atlassian, 2019).

Agile Project Manager (Agile PM): "Removes obstacles to team progress", facilitates communication and supports the team (Atlassian, 2022).

Stakeholders: This role provides input, feedback and strategic direction for the product.

UX Designer: This role "works with the product owner to come up with designs for the product" (Atlassian, 2022).

**How do these roles collaborate in Kanban vs. Scrum?**

All the roles collaborate together in Kanban and Scrum, but these are the following differences I could find, from Dovile Miseviciute (2021):

- In Kanban, the whole team helps with deciding which tasks to work on, whereas in Scrum the Product Owner (PO) is more focused on working with the Developers to make sure it matches the client's vision
- In Kanban, collaboration is continuous, whereas in Scrum the roles work together in short sprints. This also applies to stakeholder input - Scrum typically conducts a review at the end of a sprint, whilst in Kanban the feedback is continuous.
- The Scrum Master leads Agile ceremonies in Scrum

My role is Developer (Mac Developer Intern) and my role I believe is to contribute to the development of the product on the Mac platform: continuously improving the team, making sure I am delivering working code, testing, and refining.
