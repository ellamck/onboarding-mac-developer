# Agile Principles

## Research & Learn

**What is Agile, and how does it differ from traditional project management?**
Agile is a software development concept is more fluid and adaptative than traditional project management, which is very linear.

**What are the core values and principles of Agile? (Hint: Check the Agile Manifesto.)**

All below was taken from the Agile Manifesto (Beck, 2021):

Core Values:
Individuals and interactions over processes and tools
Working software over comprehensive documentation
Customer collaboration over contract negotiation
Responding to change over following a plan

Principles of Agile:

1. Highest priority is satisfy customer with early and continuous delivery
2. Welcome changing requirements
3. Deliver working software frequently
4. Business people and developers work together daily
5. Build projects around motivated individuals
6. Face-to-face conversations are the most effective method of conveying information
7. Working software is the primary measure of progress
8. Sustainable development - constant pace
9. Continuous attention to technical excellence and good design
10. Simplicity is essential
11. Best architectures, requirements, and designs emerge from self-organising teams
12. At regular intervals, reflect and adapt

**What are the differences between Scrum and Kanban, and when is each used?**

Both are Agile frameworks. All information below is from Atlassian's page (Rehkopf, 2019):

Differences:

- Scrum is used in intervals in Agile ceremonies (Sprint planning, review, retros, etc.)
- Kanban is used continuously
- Scrum is typically used by specific roles, Kanban does not require any roles
- Scrum's ideology is to learn through experiences and self organise whereas Kanban is primarily used to visualise

**Why does Focus Bear lean towards Kanban instead of Scrum?**

I believe it is because Focus Bear requires a framework that works well across multiple teams that may have different working frameworks (not all teams may adhere to Scrum). Kanban also can be used continuously, and Focus Bear's work seems to be very fast-moving, so Kanban would be better suited to the rapidly changing tasks at Focus Bear.

## Reflection

**What do you think are the biggest benefits and challenges of Agile?**

The biggest benefits of Agile I have found is the adaptability and the collaborative nature. Regular meetings make it easier to ensure there is constant progress, and it is very useful for projects where you are working with a client to produce a product for them.

One of the challenges I have experienced with Agile was working in a large team and constantly trying to find meeting times. There was 10 of us in a team, and we were all students with different timetables, so it was very hard to find standup times that worked for the team. Additionally, scope creep kept occurring due to the client changing requirements often, and we had to spend a lot of time managing that.

**How can Agile principles help improve your role and responsibilities?**

I can focus on writing clean, maintainable Swift code since Agile values working code and delivering it regularly. Agile also promotes collaboration, so being able to communicate regularly with my team will be helpful so that I can ask questions and understand what others are working on, to ensure I can achieve my tasks and support the team. As I am learning Swift for the first time, Agile's principle about continuous learning will also help, it provides me opportunity to self-organise and collaborate with mentors that are experienced with Swift.
