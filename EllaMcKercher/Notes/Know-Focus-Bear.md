**Work through the Focus Bear help centre: https://support.focusbear.io/portal/en/kb**

**Watch the Focus Bear onboarding YouTube playlists: Playlist 1 and Playlist 2.**

**Note 3-5 things about how the app is meant to work that are relevant to your role.**

1. How the app integrates with Apple's Focus mode (focus session started -> Focus mode toggles on)
2. How Focus Bear needs access to System Events and the browser for distraction blocking
3. The different window pop-ups that occur when breaks and focus sessions are completed, and how the menu bar at the top of the screen changes
4. Accessibility settings on Mac and how Focus Bear's app itself offers more fine-grained options
5. Stats, how the stats take a moment to retrieve before displaying

That you can create different Block Lists for different routines and that you can block websites but allow access to all apps. Also that micro breaks are very customisable and different from pomodoro breaks.
