# AI Usage Guidelines

## Research & Learn

**What AI tools are typically used for your role?**

Generative AI tools, those especially that assist with coding. Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini, and Copilot I believe. There is also cloud-based AI, but I am unsure if I will be using that.

**What are the benefits and risks of using AI in a professional setting?**

Benefits of using AI are, according to Melbourne Business School (“Key Findings on AI at Work and in Education,” 2025):

- Increased efficiency for mundane tasks
- Better information access (along with human oversight)
- Improved work quality (if )

Risks for using AI tools in a workplace, according to the Australian Government (“Artificial Intelligence for Small Business | Cyber.Gov.Au,” 2026):

- Data leaks and privacy breaches
- Reliability and manipulation of AI outputs

**What types of information should never be entered into AI tools?**

Any form of sensitive, personal data (defined by Australia's Privacy and Personal Information Protection Act (PPIP Act)), and proprietary code and documents (“Guide – Privacy Risks Associated with the Use of Generative AI Tools,” 2026).

**How can you fact-check and validate AI-generated content to ensure accuracy?**

- Read several sources outside of the AI output to confirm the output aligns with credible sources (Research Guides, 2026)
- Find all the different facts purported by the AI output, and examine them independently (Research Guides, 2026)

## Reflection

**When should you use AI for assistance, and when should you rely on your own skills?**

I believe for mundane tasks, AI can improve efficency - for example, turning notes into tables. However, tasks that require me to understand deeply about the content, I should rely on my own skills. AI can be used for assistance when I already have the skills to "assess the quality and accuracy of what it produces" (Responsible and Ethical Use of AI, 2026).

**How can you avoid over-reliance on AI while still benefiting from it?**

If "effort itself is the point", for example, doing my onboarding tasks to learn Swift, it would be reliance if I just used AI to do all the tasks, I wouldn't learn anything. I can still benefit by using it for mundane tasks, like generating questions, transforming information into different formats, but I should use my own skills for complicated work and for work I am assigned to that I am responsible for (Responsible and Ethical Use of AI, 2026). Ultimately, I think it is important to always have human oversight when using AI, so I should always understand what content I am generating and how I am using the AI tools.

**What steps will you take to ensure data privacy when using AI tools?**

1. Always be mindful of what data I am inputting into the AI tool, and double check before sending the input.
2. Always use anonymised data if I do have to input data (it has been approved by Focus Bear)
3. Make sure the AI tool I am using has been approved by Focus Bear, and that it has secure information practices (encryption, etc.)
