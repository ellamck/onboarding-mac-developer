# User Manual for Ella McKercher

## My role

- I am working on:
  - Mac Developer team
  - My onboarding issues
- The days and times I work are:
  - Monday, Wednesday and Friday 8:30AM to 5PM (12-12:30PM is my lunch break)

## How I Work Best

- I do my best work when:
  - I have clear tasks with known deadlines
  - I understand the tasks I am assigned and how they fit in with the overarching purpose of what I am working on
  - I have time alone to work through problems on my own, before having to present them to others or ask questions
  - I have had a good night's sleep!
  - I have time to process information and make decisions that are important
  - I can take an active break midday to go on a walk
- I struggle when:
  - I don't have time to write things down or draw out technical concepts so I can visualise them
  - I don't have time to process information and work through my understanding by myself, and I have to process information in front of people
  - I have little collaboration with others, I like to attend meetings and speak with others

## Communication Style

- I prefer messages to be...
  - Direct: easy to understand, and friendly
  - One at a time: I can get overwhelmed with too many questions/messages at once
  - If it is on Discord, during the days I work - I can get anxious if I am being messaged during my off hours (email on my off days is of course fine, or Discord in emergencies/urgent situations)
- The best way to reach me is... (e.g., Discord, email, scheduled calls)
  - Email, calls and Discord: I check email regularly, and have Focus Bear's Discord notifications on if I am tagged, so either is fine
  - I am likely to respond the fastest on Discord
  - I also am always happy to schedule and join a Google meet
- If you need something urgent, do this...
  - Call me on Discord (if during the days I work)
  - Call my phone (if during my off days)

## Collaboration & Teamwork

- I work best in a team when...
  - My teammates and I get along and I feel socially accepted
  - I understand the different roles in the team, and everyone's purpose in the team
  - I feel supported by my teammates and that I can ask them relevant questions without feeling like a burden
- My biggest pet peeve in teamwork is...
  - When people offload organisational responsibility even though it is a team effort (i.e. always letting someone else schedule meetings or consider deadlines even though it is a shared responsibility)
  - Tasks are claimed but the work is not done in a timely manner, resulting in others having to take up the slack
- If I seem quiet in a meeting, it usually means...
  - I am processing what has been said or I am trying to properly formulate any questions I have
  - I do not understand what is being said and I am lost in the conversation

## Focus & Productivity

- I stay focused by...
  - Using Focus Bear's focus sessions!
  - Maintaining organised to do lists, taking time each day to organise what I need to do
  - Taking breaks every 30 minutes or so to stretch
  - Eating at regularly scheduled times
- Things that distract me...
  - Notifications
  - Overly loud music with lyrics
  - Hunger
- The best way to support my productivity is...
  - Providing clear processes if they exist, so I can apply them later on and break them down into actionable tasks. Consistently not knowing what to do from lack of information or resources causes a lot of anxiety and cognitive load for me
  - If possible, provide clear deadlines and dependencies/reasons for deadlines early so I can prioritise accordingly

## Feedback & Growth

- I like feedback to be... (e.g., direct, gentle, with examples, public/private)
  - If it is constructive, I would prefer it to be in private so I can learn from it rather than being embarrassed
  - In a language that is clearly constructive and gentle not negative or demeaning.
  - Feedback with specific examples is very helpful for me
- When I make a mistake, the best way to let me know is...
  - In private, preferably over a message that is gentle but constructive
  - If it is a big issue, ideally over a call
- I appreciate it when teammates...
  - Make effort to get to know me a little bit
  - Are proactive and ask well-thought questions
  - Question my solutions or answers in a curious, evaluation way, not in a combative manner
