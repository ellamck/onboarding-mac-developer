# Learning Goals

## What do you hope to learn from this internship?

I am excited to work in a team to understand how a professional software team communicates and their reasoning for prioritising certain tasks. I am also eager to learn how the team splits up work, and breaks down tasks.

## What skills are you most excited to develop?

I am excited to learn Swift, as I have never used it before. I also want to learn the ettiquette Focus Bear uses for code review, and to practice writing clean, succinct code.

## What do you think you can contribute to the team?

I think I have a good understanding of why the product is important and the vision for the product, so I think when I am finished onboarding and begin coding, I think I will be able to resolve issues that align with the product's vision. I am also an active teammate, so I think I will be engaged and present in planning meetings and stand-ups, preparing ahead of time and asking questions.

## What is one area you feel less confident in that you’d like to improve?

Because I have never worked in a professional software team before, I do not want to do any faux pas or be a burden to the team, so I would like to improve my understanding of software development processes that are actually used.

I also want to become familiar and effective with Swift, as I have never learned it before and have only just begun a little before the internship commenced.

## What does success in this internship look like for you?

Gaining an understanding of how a professional software team operates, how issues are prioritised, and how the team translates the product's vision into development outcomes. Additionally, a good understanding of how to conduct myself in software team meetings, and confidence in using Swift and contributing clean, maintainable code.
