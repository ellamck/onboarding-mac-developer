# Understand Competitive Landscape

## Research Other Productivity and Focus Apps

Freedom https://freedom.to/features

- A distraction blocking application
- Can block the entire internet
- Can plan focus sessions ahead of time, recurring schedules
- Locked Mode: cannot quit a session early
- Nature sounds
- Offers real human in-app support (apparently the only screen time and focus app that does this)

Cold Turkey https://getcoldturkey.com/features/

- Blocks websites and applications
- It has granular blocking, so you can block specific URLS with wildcards, and specific YouTube channels
- Cold Turkey allows you to donate to the World Wildlife Fund for a break
- Can block via the CLI interface as well

Tiimo https://www.tiimoapp.com/product/ai-planning

- AI planner that visualises tasks
- Connects calendars, routines, tasks
- Built for those with executive functioning differences, built for neuroinclusion
- Has an AI that helps you plan and make decisions

Routinery https://www.routinery.app/

- An application for timing tasks and building routines
- Has widgets
- Website is hard to navigate
- Uses habit-stacking and audio cues
- Specifically built for people with ADHD

Notion https://www.notion.com/help/guides/what-is-notion

- Digital workspace and productivity app
- Note-taking, task management, project tracking, databases
- Has an AI that can help you build things on the platform

**What makes Focus Bear different from these apps?**

These other applications seem to have focuses on specific aspects of productivity (only distraction blocking, or only planner visualisation), but Focus Bear has several aspects of productivity embedded, like the distraction blocking, focus music, task prioritisation, habit routines, and focus sessions.

**If you were a user, why would you choose Focus Bear over competitors?**

While Tiimo is built specifically for people with ADHD, a lot of these other apps are inaccessible and not inclusive to those with differences in executive functioning. Additionally, productivity apps like Notion I think are very broad and overly customisable and they aren't conducive to building habits and focusing because of the learning curve.

I read a Reddit thread, and a user noticed they used Routinery for a long time and it worked, but out of nowhere they just stopped. I think because Focus Bear allows for customisation that isn't overwhelming, it could help with users sticking with the application for longer.

Cold Turkey's focus sessions may also be too rigid for some people - Cold Turkey allows you to donate to the World Wildlife Fund for a break, and this is very restrictive.

**What’s one feature that other apps have that Focus Bear doesn’t?**

Cold Turkey has very granular website and application blocking, for instance with YouTube, you can block specific channels.

**Based on your research, what’s one improvement you think Focus Bear could make?**

It would be good to have a planner that is more visual in the application, so I can see blocked out and colour coordinated tasks or events.
