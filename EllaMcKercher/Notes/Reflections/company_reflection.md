# Understanding Focus Bear's Mission & Vision

## Why do you think Focus Bear was created?

Presently there is such a race to be productive, and be successful _fast_. It seems like there is an oversaturation of companies and tools that try to enhance productivity with AI and algorithms, but these "solutions" are devoid of compassion and understanding of how humans work, putting productivity before the human. I think Focus Bear was made to empower creativity and curiousity, and to help its users be productive in a way that works naturally with people's needs and how their brains work.

## What problem is Focus Bear solving?

The productivity space is focused on output and success, rather than the humans behind the work itself and how productivity is achieved. There is a conflation with productivity and output, where productivity tools work against how humans naturally work, and aren't designed with science and compassion in mind. Focus Bear applies credible, proven processes and methods to help its human users channel their curiousity, creativity and ideas in a way that works with what their brains need.

## Why do you think this mission is important?

In a university class about AI ethics, I learned about the principle of Universal Design (AKA the curb-cut effect), where accessibility benefits everyone. I think Focus Bear is a great example of this, and its one of the main reasons why I think Focus Bear's mission is important. Nowadays what is expected of people is overwhelming and stressful, and I think everyone can benefit from an application like Focus Bear that prioritises our wellbeing and how we naturally operate.

Additionally, the other main reason why I think Focus Bear's mission is so important is because it is made especially for those with ADHD and neurodivergence. I have family members with ADHD, and I have seen how being forced to conform with traditional working styles is a detriment to them, and how it makes them feel like they are unmotivated, unorganised, etc. when it has nothing to do with them and their capabilities at all. Compassion in technology helps everyone and protects our wellbeing. The best way to understand something is to experience it yourself, so the fact that Focus Bear is built by people with ADHD and neurodivergence makes it all the more special and effective!

## How does Focus Bear’s work align with your personal values or interests?

As mentioned above, Focus Bear aligns with the principle of Universal Design, something I value deeply. Because I am a student in the tech/software space, I also am very interested in how to achieve a balance of productivity and wellbeing, from my own experiences struggling with it.

## Do you personally relate to any of the challenges that Focus Bear aims to solve?

Yes! I have anxiety, and I found out that when you are anxious, it can reduce your working memory, a part of your executive functioning. One thing that I have been recommended to do, which can alleviate some anxiety, is build strong routines and habits. They do not have to be rigid, but doing the same things on certain days can ease the unpredictability of the day-to-day, which helps with anxiety. Focus Bear's habit tracker and mechanisms that help you build routines without being too inflexible so far has helped me create a strong routine for my internship, and helped me avoid getting frustrated if I didn't do a task in the "perfect" way at the right time.
