# First-Time User Experience of Focus Bear

## Annotated Notes

### Focus Session Labelling

It was not fully obvious to me that once I started a focus session, that I would be able to see the focus session at the top of my screen:

<img width="277" height="16" alt="Screenshot 2026-08-14 at 14 26 21" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/eb36870d-12e4-478e-b060-e0378c512129" />

After using Focus Bear for a few hours, I then saw it, so it was not obvious to me at first. When creating your first focus session, if the app could first show you where it lives so you can easily click on it and see how much time you have been focusing for it would improve onboarding.

### Post-Break Return Button

When finishing a break, I wasn't sure what "Unlock the Machine" meant:

<img width="2363" height="1143" alt="Screenshot 2026-08-14 at 14 30 16" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/bf0665ac-afd6-4e9a-88b4-c2cfb10cfde3" />

And I was hesitant to click it, because I wasn't sure if it was making a new focus session or feature, or if it was just simply ending the break. I think clearer button labelling would help here.

### Deep Work

I didn't fully understand how to schedule Deep work, and it automatically scheduled Deep Work for me during times that didn't suit me:

<img width="824" height="635" alt="Screenshot 2026-08-14 at 14 34 17" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/3633e90f-a461-44ff-abb6-3cf04fc71221" />

I think it could help if it explained to users what the difference between deep work and working is, and how to schedule it?

### Navigating back to Focus Bear Main Dashboard

After completing a break post-focus session, I found it hard to figure out how to pull up this window:

<img width="1001" height="613" alt="Screenshot 2026-08-14 at 14 36 41" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/63716c1e-ccd7-4851-85ad-f05b221b1e6a" />

It would be good to have a button that lets you navigate from this focus session window:

<img width="317" height="572" alt="Screenshot 2026-08-14 at 14 37 47" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/b93ce14f-dc2f-4ab0-a09f-97baa30dce81" />

easily to the main dashboard. It wasn't intuitive to me to click the settings icon in order to navigate back. However, I really liked the easy to select and access tools from that small menu.
